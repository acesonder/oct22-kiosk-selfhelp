# KioskHelp - Quick Start Guide

**Version**: 1.0.0  
**For**: New Users  
**Time**: 5 minutes to get started

---

## I'm a Client - How Do I Start?

### Step 1: Register (2 minutes)

1. Go to the KioskHelp website
2. Click **"Get Started"** or **"Register"**
3. Fill out your information:
   - First Name
   - Last Name
   - Date of Birth
   - Email (optional but recommended)
   - Phone (optional)
4. Your username will be auto-generated (e.g., MICBRO050684)
5. Create a password (minimum 8 characters)
6. Choose a security question and answer
7. Read and accept the consent agreement
8. Click **"Register"**
9. You're automatically logged in!

### Step 2: Complete Your Assessment (10 minutes)

1. From your dashboard, click **"Start Assessment"**
2. Answer questions about your needs in 8 areas:
   - Housing
   - Food Security
   - Healthcare
   - Mental Health
   - Employment
   - Legal Aid
   - Transportation
   - Family Services
3. Be honest about your needs and urgency
4. Click **"Submit Assessment"**
5. The system will match you with service providers!

### Step 3: Review Your Referrals (2 minutes)

1. Click **"View Referrals"** from dashboard
2. See which providers you've been matched with
3. Review referral details
4. Wait for providers to respond (usually within 48 hours)

### Step 4: Schedule Appointments (3 minutes)

1. Once referrals are accepted, click **"My Appointments"**
2. Schedule meetings with providers
3. Add appointments to your calendar
4. Set up reminders

### Step 5: Explore Self-Help Tools

1. Click **"Self-Help Tools"** from dashboard
2. Try tools like:
   - **Budget Planner** - Manage your finances
   - **Wellness Tracker** - Monitor your mood and health
   - **Goal Planner** - Set and achieve goals
   - **Job Search Organizer** - Track job applications
   - And more!

## I'm Staff - How Do I Start?

### Step 1: Get Your Account

1. Contact your administrator for staff account credentials
2. You'll receive:
   - Username
   - Temporary password (change on first login)

### Step 2: Login

1. Go to **Staff Portal** (staff/login.php)
2. Enter your username and password
3. Access your staff dashboard

### Step 3: View Your Caseload

1. Dashboard shows your assigned clients
2. See today's appointments
3. View pending tasks

### Step 4: Manage Cases

1. Click on a client to view their case
2. Review their assessment
3. Check active referrals
4. Add case notes
5. Track progress

### Step 5: Create Referrals

1. Select a client
2. Click **"Create Referral"**
3. Choose service type
4. System suggests matching providers
5. Set priority level
6. Add notes
7. Submit referral

---

## I'm a Provider - How Do I Start?

### Step 1: Get Your Account

1. Register your organization with the system
2. Administrator will create your provider account
3. Receive login credentials

### Step 2: Login

1. Go to **Provider Portal** (provider/login.php)
2. Enter your credentials
3. Access provider dashboard

### Step 3: Review Referral Queue

1. Dashboard shows incoming referrals
2. Review client needs
3. Check your availability

### Step 4: Accept or Decline Referrals

1. Click on a referral to review details
2. **Accept** if you can serve the client
3. **Decline** if unable (suggest alternatives)
4. Respond within 48 hours

### Step 5: Schedule Appointments

1. Contact accepted clients
2. Schedule initial appointment
3. Update referral status
4. Provide services

## I'm an Admin - How Do I Start?

### Step 1: Initial Setup

1. Navigate to **Admin Panel** (admin/login.php)
2. Enter admin code: **079777**
3. ⚠️ **IMPORTANT**: Change this code immediately in production!

### Step 2: Initialize Database

1. From admin dashboard, go to **Database Tools**
2. Click **"Test Connection"** - ensure database is accessible
3. Click **"Initialize Database"**
4. Confirm action (this creates all tables)
5. Wait for completion message

### Step 3: Configure System

1. Go to **System Configuration**
2. Update:
   - Site name
   - Contact information
   - Time zone
   - Security settings
3. Save changes

### Step 4: Create User Accounts

1. Go to **User Management**
2. Click **"Add New User"**
3. Create accounts for:
   - Staff members
   - Service providers
   - Test clients (for training)

### Step 5: Customize Branding

1. Go to **Theme Customization**
2. Upload your logo
3. Set color scheme
4. Customize welcome message
5. Preview and save

## Quick Tips

### For Clients:

✅ **Save your username** - Write it down, you'll need it to login  
✅ **Remember your security answer** - Needed for password recovery  
✅ **Keep email updated** - For important notifications  
✅ **Check messages regularly** - Staff and providers may contact you  
✅ **Update assessment** - When your needs change  
✅ **Use self-help tools** - They help track progress  
✅ **Don't share password** - Keep your account secure

### For Staff:

✅ **Document everything** - Add case notes for all interactions  
✅ **Follow up on referrals** - Check status regularly  
✅ **Respond to messages** - Within 24-48 hours  
✅ **Update cases** - Keep information current  
✅ **Coordinate with providers** - Good communication is key  
✅ **Set reminders** - For follow-ups and appointments

### For Providers:

✅ **Respond quickly** - To referrals within 48 hours  
✅ **Keep services updated** - Current availability and capacity  
✅ **Accept only if you can serve** - Better to decline than fail to deliver  
✅ **Suggest alternatives** - When declining referrals  
✅ **Communicate clearly** - With clients and staff  
✅ **Update referral status** - Keep everyone informed

### For Admins:

✅ **Backup regularly** - Daily backups recommended  
✅ **Monitor error logs** - Catch issues early  
✅ **Update documentation** - When making changes  
✅ **Train users** - Ensure everyone knows the system  
✅ **Change default credentials** - Security first!  
✅ **Test before deploying** - Especially updates
